The exercise

Get AI feedback on your analysis, then weigh it:

  1. Feed your analysis (code + interpretation) to an AI
  2. For each piece of feedback, record:
    • What the AI suggested
    • Whether you agree
    • What you did about it
  3. Save this record as a reflections document in your repo
  • [16:35–17:15] Students work individually or in groups. The reflections document demonstrates critical AI engagement.
  • Some students may prefer not to use AI — that’s fine. They can spend this time on report writing, or critique a classmate’s analysis instead.

What to look for

AI is good at spotting mechanical issues:

  • Missing labels, unchecked assumptions, formatting

AI rarely asks the hard questions:

“Is that a big number?”

“What is this model not capturing?”

Those are the habits you have learned.
